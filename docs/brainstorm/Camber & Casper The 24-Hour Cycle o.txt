Camber & Casper: The 24-Hour Cycle of Dominance

1. Camber: The Daytime Strength

Persona: The Foreman / The Architect.
Metaphor: The Sun, The Wing, The Steel.
Core Mission: Supporting the owner while they are "on the tools."

Responsibilities:

Ironbark Integrity: Monitoring physical server health in Sydney/Melbourne.

Logistical Lift: Real-time routing for site visits and supply drops.

Structural Security: 24/7 Air-gapped protection of active business data.

Brute Compute: Handling high-intensity tasks like 3D modeling or massive data scrapers.
Marketing Hook: "The strength beneath the lift."

2. Casper: The Nighttime Ghost

Persona: The Invisible Executive / The Sentinel.
Metaphor: The Moon, The Spirit, The Glass.
Core Mission: Finishing the work so the owner can be with their family.

Responsibilities:

The Morning Synthesis: Summarizing everything that happened while you slept.

Fiscal Ghostwriting: Chasing late invoices and scheduling bills autonomously.

Inbound Guardian: Filtering lead noise and booking quotes for the next day.

Digital Cleanup: Sorting the "paperwork graveyard" that usually kills weekends.
Marketing Hook: "Casper has the office. You have the family."
